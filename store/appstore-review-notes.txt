Mùa Lúa Chín - Notes for App Review

No account or demo login is needed. The whole game plays offline: open the
app and the guide quests walk you through planting, harvesting and
delivering orders. Signing in is optional and only
used for cloud save and the online features (neighbours, market, chat,
leaderboards).

About the game
Mùa Lúa Chín ("Ripe Rice Season") is an original, relaxing farm game set in a
village of northern Vietnam. All buildings, crops, animals, villagers,
scenery and music were made for this game; nothing is licensed or reskinned
from another title. Content includes:
- Rice paddies, vegetable beds, orchards, pens, fish ponds, silkworms and
  village craft workshops (rice mill, rice-cake kitchen, loom house...).
- The game follows the real Vietnamese lunar calendar: 24 solar terms, crop
  seasons, weather, moon phases and 7 traditional festivals on their lunar
  dates, plus a rice-drying yard that must be covered before rain.
- Village projects (well, gate, road, school, clinic, communal house), a
  painted "Village Memories" album, weekly events and river fishing with a
  weekly fishing leaderboard.
The interface is in Vietnamese; the game's primary audience is Vietnam.

Sign-in, data and account deletion
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In are offered side by side (the ☰ menu
  button > "Lưu trữ đám mây" / Cloud save).
- Account deletion is in the app: menu > Lưu trữ đám mây > "Xóa tài khoản và
  dữ liệu trên mây" (Delete account and cloud data). It deletes the cloud
  save, the village on the region map, market stalls, leaderboard rows and
  the sign-in account.
- Signed-in players are shown to others only by the farm name they choose
  (no email or account name). "Ẩn tôi khỏi bảng" (Hide me) in the
  leaderboard screen removes their rows.

World chat (Kênh Thế Giới)
- Only signed-in players can post. Messages are filtered for profanity, a
  short cooldown limits flooding, and messages are deleted after 3 days.
- Tap any message from another player to Report it or Block its sender. Reports
  are reviewed by the developer, and offending accounts are muted.

In-app purchases (shop button on the farm screen, or ☰ menu > "Cửa hàng,
khôi phục giao dịch" / Shop, restore purchases)
- Starter Pack, Laterite Cellar and Remove Ads are non-consumable; the
  30-day Farmer Card is consumable. "Khôi phục giao dịch" (Restore
  Purchases) is at the bottom of the Shop.

Ads
- Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always the player's choice (finish a crop
  or workshop now, double level-up coins). An interstitial may follow every
  third delivered order from level 4, at most once every 5 minutes, and an
  App Open ad may show at launch or when returning after 30 minutes away
  (from level 3). Remove Ads turns both off.
- App Tracking Transparency is requested before the first ad request. Ads
  still work, non-personalised, if the player declines.
